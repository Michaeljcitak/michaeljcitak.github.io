Support & Privacy — Games by Michael J. Citak

One page covering eleven games: Chronies: The Golden Rule, Cosmic Quiz, Isles of Spectralia, Witherspoon Falls, Secondborn, Luna and the Heartstone, Isles of Spectralia: Side Scroller, and Sherlock Holmes' Legacy, and Blackmoor Manor, and Blood & Thunder, and The Lost Kingdoms of Oz.
Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Developer: Michael J. Citak · Contact: michaeljcitak@gmail.com

Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback about any of the eleven games: michaeljcitak@gmail.com

To help us help you, include:

  • The game's name (Chronies: The Golden Rule, Cosmic Quiz, Isles of Spectralia, Witherspoon Falls, Secondborn, Luna and the Heartstone, Isles of Spectralia: Side Scroller, Sherlock Holmes' Legacy, or Blackmoor Manor)
  • Your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The game's version (shown on its title or credits screen)
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen

Every message is read. These are small, independently made games, so replies come from the developer personally — please allow a few days.

Quick answers

  • Do the games need an internet connection? Cosmic Quiz, Isles of Spectralia, Witherspoon Falls, Secondborn, Luna and the Heartstone, Isles of Spectralia: Side Scroller, Sherlock Holmes' Legacy, and Blackmoor Manor work fully offline. Chronies plays fully offline too; two optional links inside it (a video reel and the Showrunner site) open in your browser only if you tap them.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device, inside the game itself. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app deletes its saved progress. (Secondborn, Luna and the Heartstone, Isles of Spectralia: Side Scroller, Sherlock Holmes' Legacy, and Blackmoor Manor are the exceptions: they save nothing at all — closing the app restarts the game.)
  • Do the games have ads or in-app purchases? The games show no third-party advertising and include no advertising or analytics SDKs. Any purchase options, if ever offered, would be described here first.

Privacy Policy

The short version: these games collect nothing. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and nothing is sent to any server — ours or anyone else's. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing being collected.

What all eleven games share

  • Each game runs entirely on your device.
  • None of them asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • None of them includes third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or social-login services.
  • None of them creates a profile of you, on the device or anywhere else.

Chronies: The Golden Rule

  • Your story progress — your team, your discoveries, your place in the adventure — is stored only in the game's local storage on your device. It never leaves the device.
  • The game's in-story notices and house announcements are part of the game itself, displayed on your device. Their display counts are kept on the device only; nothing about your play is reported anywhere.
  • Two links inside the game (a Facebook video reel and the Showrunner website) open in your device's browser only when you tap them. Those websites are run by their respective operators under their own privacy policies; the game sends them nothing about you.
  • The guardian Birth Certificate keepsake may show a name you choose to write on it. That name stays on your device unless you deliberately share the keepsake image using your device's share sheet — sharing is always your action, never the game's.

Cosmic Quiz

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline.
  • Scores and the Space Cadet Hall of Fame exist only for the current play session, on your device. When the session ends, they are gone.
  • Answers you type for Cosmic Conundrum questions are checked on the device and are never transmitted or stored anywhere.

Isles of Spectralia

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app.)
  • Game state exists only for the current play session, on your device. Nothing about your duels, decks, or collection is transmitted or stored anywhere else.

Witherspoon Falls

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app.)
  • Your saga progress and Case Board evidence are stored only in the game's local storage on your device. They never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Secondborn

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app.)
  • Progress is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the story.
  • The credits page carries one optional link to the novel's Amazon page. It opens in your device's browser only when you tap it; that website is run by its operator under its own privacy policy, and the game sends it nothing about you.

Luna and the Heartstone

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app — it has no external links of any kind.)
  • Progress in both quests is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the adventure.
  • The Guardian keepsake pictures are drawn on your device and saved only when the player taps save. Sharing one is always the player's action, never the game's.

Isles of Spectralia: Side Scroller

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its artwork is built into the app.)
  • Progress is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the adventure.
  • The game contains no external links. Its credits mention the Gertrude Lives Her Best Life webseries page (facebook.com/gertrudelovesUO) in plain text only — it is not a link and opens nothing.

Sherlock Holmes' Legacy

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app.)
  • Progress is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the case.
  • The game carries one optional link to the novels' Amazon page, on the hub poster and in the credits. It opens in your device's browser only when you tap it; that website is run by its operator under its own privacy policy, and the game sends it nothing about you.

Blackmoor Manor

  • The game makes no network requests at all and works fully offline. (All of its fonts and artwork are built into the app.)
  • Progress is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the saga.
  • The game carries no external links of any kind. The novels it is adapted from are credited as text on its credits page only.
  • Blackmoor Manor is a gothic horror saga written for teen and adult players; it is not directed at children.

Blood & Thunder

  • The game runs fully offline: all of its artwork, songs and fonts are built into the app, and it makes no network requests on its own.
  • Progress is held only in memory for the current play session — nothing is stored or transmitted anywhere. Closing the app restarts the show.
  • The app carries two optional links to the outside, each opened only when you tap it: the Suno playlist of the author's own recordings and the author's contact email on the Production & Licensing page. Those destinations are run by their operators under their own privacy policies, and the app sends them nothing about you. The "Watch the Show" button never leaves the app — the Animated Musical is built into the same app and plays inside it.
  • Blood & Thunder is a musical about John Brown and the coming of the Civil War, written for teen and adult audiences; it is not directed at children.

The Lost Kingdoms of Oz

  • One app, two ways in: a chooser opens either the playable game or the Animated Musical watch edition. The app runs fully offline: all of its artwork, songs and fonts are built into the app, and it makes no network requests on its own. Moving between the chooser, the game and the musical never leaves the app.
  • In the game, your progress is stored only in the game's local storage on your device. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it. The musical keeps no state at all.
  • The app carries three optional links to the outside, each opened only when you tap it: the Suno page for the author's own recordings of the songs, the Amazon listing for the author's libretto, and the author's contact email on the game's Production & Licensing page. Those destinations are run by their operators under their own privacy policies, and the app sends them nothing about you.
  • The Lost Kingdoms of Oz is a family musical adventure; it is not part of the "Made for Kids" category.

Children

Because the games collect no personal information from anyone, they collect no personal information from children either.
